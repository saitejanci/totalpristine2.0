About Total Pristine

We take pride in transforming outdoor and exterior spaces into clean, tidy, and welcoming environments.

Whether it’s refreshing a tired garden, restoring a dirty patio, or keeping your property looking sharp all year round, we focus on attention to detail and delivering visible results.

From small residential jobs to full exterior clean-ups, our goal is simple: leave every space looking pristine.
